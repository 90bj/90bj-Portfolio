03 — How it works

From sketch to in-game.

  1. 01

    Concept

    We lock the brief: silhouette, theme, colour and where it sits on the avatar.

  2. 02

    Sculpt & model

    High-detail sculpt in Blender, then a clean low-poly build that holds the shape.

  3. 03

    Bake & texture

    Normals, colour and gradients baked down so it reads as rich at tiny sizes.

  4. 04

    Test & ship

    Checked in Roblox Studio against limits, then delivered upload-ready.
